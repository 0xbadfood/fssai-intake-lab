# How to apply license for Airport/Seaport?

**Step 1:** Refer to How to apply for license/registration manual on https://foscoss.fssai.gov.in/

**Step 2:** Click on License/Registration and Apply for License/Registration on the yellow Menu box.

[Image: A screenshot of a web interface. The top navigation bar has buttons: 'Track Application', 'Know Your Officer', 'FBO Search', 'How to Apply', 'Application Processing', 'License Kit'. Below, there is a form with fields 'Enter Application Reference No.' and 'Enter Captcha Code' (showing '576406' and a refresh icon), and a 'Submit' button. To the right, text reads 'Filling Application for Registration becomes easier through App. Remain updated with FSSAI news. To download 'Food Safety Connect' App, Scan the below QR' next to a QR code. Below the form, there are five colored boxes: an orange box 'Apply License/Registration Fee: Rs.100 to 7500 per year', a green box 'Modification of License and Registration Fee: Rs.1000 for License Rs.100 for Registration', a light blue box 'Renewals Fee is same as of new License and Registration. Can be applied as early as 180 days prior to expiry date', a dark blue box 'Food Consumer Grievance', and a purple box 'File Excess Annual Return'.]

**Step 3:** Click on Apply for License/Registration on the blue Box.

[Image: A screenshot of a webpage. The header shows logos for 'fssai', 'FoSCoS Food Safety Compliance System', and 'Eat Right India'. Navigation links include 'Home', 'About FoSCoS', 'FSSAI', 'User Manual', 'Login-Businesses', 'Login-Authorities'. A large teal banner reads 'Apply for New License/Registration'. Below are three buttons: a red button 'Apply For License/Registration', a green button 'Apply For License/Registration [Railways]', and a light blue button 'Apply For License/Registration [Airport/Seaport]'. The footer has columns: 'DOCUMENTS REQUIRED' (with 'NEW/RENEWAL/MODIFICATION OF LICENSE'), 'ADDITIONAL INFORMATION' (with 'ANNUAL RETURN FORM D-1'), 'FSS ACT, 2006', 'FSS RULES, 2011', 'USEFUL LINKS' (with 'FoStac', 'Audit Management System').]

**Step 4:** Select the Airport /seaport and Read the Note before proceeding.
