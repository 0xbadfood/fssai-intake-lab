How to apply for a license of E-commerce business?
Revised- How to apply for a license for an E-commerce business?

**Step 1:** Refer to How to apply for license/registration manual on https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/

**Step 2:** Click on License/Registration and Apply for License/Registration on the yellow Menu box. Next Click on Apply for License/Registration on the Red Box.
