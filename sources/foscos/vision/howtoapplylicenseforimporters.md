# How to apply license for Importers?

**Step 1:** Refer to How to apply for license/registration manual on https://foscoss.fssai.gov.in/

**Step 2:** Click on License/Registration and Apply for License/Registration on the yellow Menu box.

[Image of a web interface. Top navigation bar has buttons: 'Track Application', 'Know Your Officer', 'FBO Search', 'How to Apply', 'Application Processing', 'License Kit'. To the right, text: 'Filling Application for Registration becomes easier through App. Remain updated with FSSAI news. To download Food Safety Connect App Scan the below QR' with a QR code. Can be applied as early as 180 days prior to expiry date'), Blue ('Food Consumer Grievance'), Dark Blue ('File Express Annual Return').]

**Step 3:** Click on Apply for License/Registration on the Red Box.

<!-- page 2 -->

**Step 4: Select the State and Read the Note before proceeding.**

Select Kind of Business

Select the state where premises is located for which License/Registration will be obtained. [Select State ▼]

Note: Each location will be issued separate license (except transporter where one license will be issued for all vehicle of a single transporter/ business). In case FBO is operating in more than one state, he has to obtain one additional Central License for Head office/ registered office and separate license for each location as per Capacity/Turnover (State/ Central License)

A Food Business Operator (FBO)'s premise shall have only one FSSAI License or Registration on which any number of kind of businesses (KoB) can be endorsed.

<!-- page 3 -->

**Step 5:** Read the Group Heads of Kind of Business and select Importer under trade/retail.

[Image: A horizontal navigation bar with tabs: 'Manufacturer', 'Trade/Retail' (highlighted in red), 'Food Services', 'Central Govt. Agencies', 'Head office'. Below this, a list of selectable options in teal boxes: 'Storage (Except Controlled Atmosphere and Cold)', 'Storage (Cold / Refrigerated)', 'Storage (Controlled Atmosphere + Cold)', 'Wholesaler', 'Distributor', 'Transportation (having a number of specialized vehicles like insulated refrigerated van/ wagon and milk tankers etc.)', 'Retailer', 'Food Vending Agencies', 'Importer'. The 'Importer' option is expanded, showing a definition: 'Import is an activity where articles of trade from a foreign source is brought into a domestic market in the course of trade.' and a checkbox labeled 'Importer'. A note follows: 'Note: To apply under importer category FBO must have IE Code from DGFT. The address of premises in case of importer should be same as mentioned in IE Code. Even if importer is having multiple premises, only 1(One) licence is applicable, if applying for Importer Kind of Business only.']

**Step 6:** Under Importers KOB, read the definition, click on the radio button to apply for the central license, and click on Proceed.

[Image: A form section titled 'Importers'. It contains the definition: 'Import is an activity where articles of trade from a foreign source is brought into a domestic market in the course of trade.' A checkbox is selected next to 'Importer'. A note is present: 'Note: To apply under importer category FBO must have IE Code from DGFT. The address of premises in case of importer should be same as mentioned in IE Code. Even if importer is having multiple premises, only 1(One) licence is applicable, if applying for Importer Kind of Business only.' Below are three buttons: 'Clear Selected KOBs', 'Proceed', 'Cancel'.]

**Step 7:** Fill in all the mandatory fields as shown in Form B. Please fill the communication details with caution as all the communication made by the authority will be done to the mentioned contact details viz Mobile No. or Email Id etc. Fill GST/PAN/CIN in the relevant fields as applicable to your firm/company.
