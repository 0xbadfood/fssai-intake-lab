# How to apply license for exporters?

**Step 1:** Refer to How to apply for license/registration manual on https://foscoss.fssai.gov.in/

**Step 2:** Click on License/Registration and Apply for License/Registration on the yellow Menu box.

**Step 3:** Click on Apply for License/Registration on the Red Box.

Navigation links: 'Home | About FoSCoS | FSSAI | User Manual | Login-Businesses | Login-Authorities'. <!-- page 2 -->

**Step 4:** Select the State and Read the Note before proceeding.

Select Kind of Business

Select the state where premises is located for which License/Registration will be obtained. [Select State ▼]

Note: Each location will be issued separate license (except transporter where one license will be issued for all vehicle of a single transporter/ business). In case FBO is operating in more than one state, he has to obtain one additional Central License for Head office/ registered office and separate license for each location as per Capacity/Turnover (State/ Central License)

A Food Business Operator (FBO)'s premise shall have only one FSSAI License or Registration on which any number of kind of businesses (KoB) can be endorsed.

**Step 5:** Read the Group Heads of Kind of Business and select Exporters under Manufacturer.

[Image of a form interface. Top section shows a horizontal navigation bar with tabs: 'Manufacturer' (selected, red underline), 'trade/retail', 'Food Services', 'Central Govt. Agencies', 'head office'. Below the tabs, a descriptive text reads: 'If manufacturing/processing Dairy products, Vegetable Oil, Meat Products, Novel food products and all food processing units including repackers.' Below this is a list of selectable options, each in a teal-colored bar with a plus icon on the right: 'Dairy units', 'Vegetable oil and processing units', 'Slaughtering units', 'Meat processing units', 'Food or Health Supplements and Nutraceuticals etc.', 'General Manufacturing', 'Proprietary Food', 'Non-specified food and food ingredients', 'Substances Added to Food', 'Radiation Processing of Food', '100 % Export Oriented units', 'Exporter - Manufacturer' (with a question mark icon). At the bottom are three buttons: 'Clear Selected IOBs', 'Proceed', 'Cancel'.]

**Step 6:** Under Exporters there are three types of kind of business.

| Kind of Business | Central License | State License | Registration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 100 % Export Oriented units | 100 % Export Oriented Units | Not Applicable | Not Applicable |
| Exporter - Manufacturer | Only Exports | Not Applicable | Not Applicable |
