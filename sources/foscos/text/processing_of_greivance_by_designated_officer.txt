How to process the complaint?
Step 1: Login to the portal foscos.fssai.gov.in and navigate to “Food Concern
Management”. Under it click on “Pending Complaints”.




                                                                     Click Here




Step 2: Search for the ticket number against which action is to be taken or navigate
manually and click on “Proceed”.
Step 3: Scroll down to “Action To be Performed by Designated Officer”




Action 1: Resolve Consumer Grievance – If the complaint pertains to the designated officer
then they can click on this radio button, fill in the remarks and click on “Submit” button to
resolve the complaint from their end.




Action 2: If the complaint does not pertain to them then the designated officer can transfer
the complaint to the designated officer to whom the complaint is escalated.
For this, click on the “Transfer To Other DO” radio button, choose the district, sub-district
(If required), fill remarks and click on “Submit” button to resolve the complaint from their
end.




NOTE: Complaints will be visible in “Closed Complaint” bin once closed by the consumer
