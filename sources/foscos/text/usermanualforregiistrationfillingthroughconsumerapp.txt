1.User manual of Registration application filing through Consumer
App for FBO:-

Step 0: Users can log on to this URL and download the Android application.
      https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=safety.connect.food
Step 1: Open food safety connect app by clicking on icon.
Step 2:Click on apply for registration orange colour button on first screen.
Step 3: Click on apply for registration orange colour button on first screen.




Step 4: Click on drop down and select your state and KOB’s
Step 5: Fill all required registration details to file the registration through app and
process.




Step 6: Fill primary contact details and secondary contact details along with
password and click on submit.
Step 7: Fill OTP received on your contact number and email id. A dialogue will open
up read the instructions, then press ok
Step 8: Upload all required documents like photo,ID proof and other documents.




Step 9: Click on preview application and you can see all details filled by you till now
and download application form.
Step 10: Pay amount of registration by selecting any payment mode and download
receipt.
