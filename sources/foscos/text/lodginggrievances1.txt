How to file a complaint?
Step 1: Navigate to Food Safety Connect portal and login to your account.




Login
here




Step 2: On the left panel, navigate to “Register New Complaint”.




           Click here
Step 3: Select category from the dropdown and based on the category continue to fill all
the mandatory complaint details.
Step 4: Selection of Food Business Operator - please select Food Business Operator if
available in the dropdown list, if not then select “Other FBO”.




Step 5: After entering all the details correctly, click on “Submit” button at the bottom of the
page.
Step 6: A ticket number will be generated against the complaint through which consumer
can track the complaint.
