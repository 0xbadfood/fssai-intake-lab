How to apply for a license for Restaurant?


Step 1: Refer to How to apply for license /registration manual for creating a
license/registration

Step 2: Click on License/Registration and Apply for License/Registration on the yellow Menu
box.




Step 3: Select the State and Read the Note before proceeding.




Step 4: Read the Group Heads of Kind of Business. Select Restaurant from food services
Business Group Head.
Step 5: Choose the right Radio Button according to the turnover of your food business and
Proceed to fill out the application.




 Step 6: Fill in all the mandatory fields as shown in Form B. Please fill the communication
 details with caution as all the communication made by the authority will be done to the
 mentioned contact details viz Mobile No. or Email Id etc.
Step 7: Upload the relevant set of documents.
Step 8: Pay the fee with available modes and apply.




Step 9: After successfully completing the payment, a receipt will be generated with a
17-digit reference number which can be used for future reference.
