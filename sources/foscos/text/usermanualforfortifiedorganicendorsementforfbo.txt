1.User manual of Fortified/Organic endorsement for FBO:-

Step 0: Users can log on to this URL https://foscos.fssai.gov.in on the web browser.
Sign in using Application Reference No/license No.




Step 1: - Select the licence/registration menu and then click on the
Endorsement(Organic) sub-menu or Endorse Fortified Products as applicable.
Step 2:In Organic Endorsement - Select any license and click on proceed and fill all
required details, fill brand name and submit. Organic endorsement successful pop up
comes up.




Step 3: In Fortified Endorsement - Select any license and click on proceed and fill
all required details, fill brand name and submit. Fortified endorsement successful
pop up comes up.
