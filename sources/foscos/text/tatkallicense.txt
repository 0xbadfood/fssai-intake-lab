                   USER MANUAL FOR TATKAL LICENSE/REGISTRATION



1. Goto FoSCoS Home Page and click on “Apply for Tatkal License/Registration”




2. The following screenshot shows the Eligible KoB list
3. Select KoB from the Eligible KoB list and click on proceed
4. Click on proceed




5. Enter the GST number and click on proceed
6. Fill the premises details and click on Verify PAN




7. Enter the required PAN details and click on Submit
8. PAN details verified successfully.




9. Select the products and click on Save & Next
10. Enter the details for Sign Up and Click on Submit




11. On successful Signup, the login ID is generated.
12. Enter the Communication details.
13. Enter the OTP received on both - Mobile and Aadhar registered mobile and click on Submit.




14. Upload all the mandatory documents.
15. Enter the OTP received on Aadhar registered mobile number




16. Select the payment method
17. E-Sign using Aadhar – Enter the Aadhar number and click on proceed.




18. Click on Pay and proceed with the payment.
19. After successful payment, Login to FoSCoS and download the issued License copy from the
    “Issued” bin.
