   How can I check if the endorsement application status?

Step 1: The FBO will have to log into the FoSCoS website (https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/ ) using their
credentials. After logging in, navigate to License/Registration and click on “Endorse Fortified Products”.
Step 2: Click on “History” to check if the endorsement application has been submitted
The History window will pop up, showing the status of your endorsement application. If the Status shows
“Pending for approval from competent authority”, it suggests that your endorsement application has
been submitted but yet to be approved by competent authority.
The status will reflect as “Accepted”, once the endorsement application has been accepted and approved by the
competent authority. The FBO can close the “History” window and click on “view Fortification” to download their
endorsement certificate.
