                      USER MANUAL FOR DIRECT SELLER LICENSE



1. Goto FoSCoS Home Page and click on “Apply for New License/Registration” then click on
   “General”
2. Select the state from the drop-down menu
3. Select the Direct Seller under the Trade/Retail tab, choose the correct option as per
   turnover and click on “Proceed”




4. Click on “Proceed” to fill the application
5. Fill the required details
6. Set the premise location accurately




7. Select the products (under Direct Seller) and click on Save & Next
8. Enter the details for Sign Up and Click on Submit
9. Enter the OTP received on both- Mobile and email and click on Submit.




10. On successful Signup, the login ID is generated.
11. Enter the Communication details.




12. Select the Number of years for the License
13. Upload all the mandatory documents.




14. Click on Save & Next
15. E-Sign using Aadhar/Mobile – Enter the Mobile/Aadhar number and click on “proceed”.




16. Select the payment method
17. Proceed with the payment.




18. Application receipt is generated.
19. The status of the application will be Stage 2: Document Scrutinization”.
