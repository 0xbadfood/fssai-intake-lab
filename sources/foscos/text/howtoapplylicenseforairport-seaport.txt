How to apply license for Airport/Seaport?

    Step 1: Refer to How to apply for license/registration manual on https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/

    Step 2: Click on License/Registration and Apply for License/Registration on the yellow Menu box.




      Step 3: Click on Apply for License/Registration on the blue Box.




Step 4: Select the Airport /seaport and Read the Note before proceeding.
Step 5: Read the Group Heads of Kind of Business and select the appropriate Kind of Business.
Under Trade/retail and food services group head there are different Kind of businesses as below:
Step 6: Read the notice carefully and proceed further.
 Step 7: Fill in all the mandatory fields as shown in Form B. Please fill the communication details with
caution as all the communication made by the authority will be done to the mentioned contact details
viz Mobile No. or Email Id etc. Fill GST/PAN/CIN in the relevant fields as applicable to your
firm/company.
Step 8: Upload the relevant set of documents.




  Step 9: Pay the fee with available modes and apply.
Step 10: After completing the payment, a receipt will be generated with a 17-digit reference number
which can be used for future reference.




Step 11: User can track the status of application through the Homepage i.e. https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/
