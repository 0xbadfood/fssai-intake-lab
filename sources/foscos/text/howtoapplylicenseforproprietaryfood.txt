How to apply license for Proprietary food?
NOTE: License for proprietary food is only valid if FBO conforms to sub- Regulation 2.12 of Food Safety
and Standards (Food Products Standards andFood Additives) Amendment Regulations].

    Step 1: Refer to How to apply for license/registration manual on https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/

    Step 2: Click on License/Registration and Apply for License/Registration on the yellow Menu box.




     Step 3: Click on Apply for License/Registration on the Red Box.
Step 4: Select the State and Read the Note before proceeding.




Step 5: Read the Group Heads of Kind of Business{Manufacturer} and select Proprietary Kind of Business.




Step 6: Fill in all the mandatory fields as shown in Form B. Please fill the communication details with
caution as all the communication made by the authority will be done to the mentioned contact details
viz Mobile No. or Email Id etc. Fill GST/PAN/CIN in the relevant fields as applicable to your
firm/company.
Step 7: Upload the relevant set of documents.
 Step 8: Pay the fee with available modes and apply.




Step 9: After completing the payment, a receipt will be generated with a 17-digit reference number
which can be used for future reference.
Step 10: User can track the status of application through the Homepage i.e.https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/
