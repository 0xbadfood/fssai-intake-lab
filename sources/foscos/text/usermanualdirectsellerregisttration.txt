                   USER MANUAL FOR DIRECT SELLER REGISTRATION

1. Goto FoSCoS Home Page and click on “Apply for New License/Registration” then click on
   “General”
2. Select the state from the drop-down menu




3. Select the Direct Seller under the Trade/Retail tab, choose the correct option as per
   turnover and click on “Proceed”
4. Click on “Proceed” to fill the application.




5. Fill the required details
6. Select the products and click on “proceed”




7. Enter the details for Sign Up and Click on Submit
8. Enter the OTP received on Mobile / email and click on Submit.




9. On successful Signup, the login ID is generated.
10. Upload all the mandatory documents.




11. Select the payment method
12. E-Sign using Mobile – Enter the Mobile number and click “proceed”.




13. Proceed with the payment.
14. The status of the application will be Stage 1: Processing to be initiated”.
