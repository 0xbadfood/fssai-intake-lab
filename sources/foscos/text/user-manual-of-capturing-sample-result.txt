1. The User manual of Capturing sample result for all Authorities:-

Note:- Capturing sample result provided by FoSCoS for every authorities flow will
be the same as shown below.

Step 0: Authorities can log on to this URL https://foscos.fssai.gov.in on the web
browser. Sign in using username and password.




Step 1: Select the License Inspection/Registration Inspection menu and then click
on Sample details without inspection sub-menu.

Note:- Users can add License/Registration no and click on the search button.
Step 2: Click on Add sample collection.




Step 2: Fill license Number and click on the get details button. All details will
autofill and click on ‘submit sample’, after that sample detail form will open fill form
and upload a document as per requirement.
Step 3: All filed Samples will be listed in the record table.
