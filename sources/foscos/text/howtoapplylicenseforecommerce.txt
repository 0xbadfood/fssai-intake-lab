  How to apply for a license of E-commerce business?
  Revised- How to apply for a license for an E-commerce business?


  Step 1: Refer to How to apply for license/registration manual on https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/

   Step 2: Click on License/Registration and Apply for License/Registration on the yellow Menu box. Next
   Click on Apply for License/Registration on the Red Box.




Step 3: Select the State and Read the Note before proceeding.
Step 4: Read the Group Heads of Kind of Business and select Trade/retail and Under E-Commerce read
the definition, click on the radio button to apply for a central license, and click on Proceed.
Step 5: Fill in all the mandatory fields as shown in Form B. Please fill the communication details with
caution as all the communication made by the authority will be done to the mentioned contact details
viz Mobile No. or Email Id etc. Fill GST/PAN/CIN in the relevant fields as applicable to your
firm/company.
 Step 6: Upload the relevant set of documents.




Step 7: Pay the fee with available modes and apply.
Step 8: After completing the payment, a receipt will be generated with a 17-digit reference number
which can be used for future reference.
Step 9: User can track the status of application through the Homepage i.e.
https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/
